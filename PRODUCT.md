# Product

## What it is
The portfolio and lead-generation site of ZACK, a software engineer who builds websites, apps and
desktop software for businesses.

## Who it's for
1. **Business owners** (shops, restaurants, cafés, companies, clinics), mostly in Algeria and the
   Arab and French-speaking world, plus international clients. Many browse on phones. They want to
   know: what can he build, has he built real things, what does it cost roughly, how do I reach him.
2. **Referrers**: people who know a business that needs software and want the referral reward.
3. **The owner**, who must be able to update everything without touching code.

## Jobs the site must do
- Make a strong, memorable first impression (3D hero) without slowing the page down.
- Show real work honestly: real screenshots, truthful status (Concept / In progress / Launched),
  clear Client / Personal / Concept labels.
- Explain services and how pricing works; show prices only once the owner confirms them.
- Turn interest into an inquiry (contact form, email, phone, WhatsApp) or a referral.
- Work in 13 languages, including Arabic right-to-left.

## Rules (from the owner's brief)
- Never invent facts, clients, results, testimonials, prices or experience.
- Business improvement is a goal, never a guarantee.
- Maintenance = bug fixes in the delivered scope; new features need a new estimate.
- First 6 months of domain + hosting included; renewals paid by the client.
- Concept visuals are always labelled; everything public is editable or removable in the admin.

## Tone
Direct, plain and confident. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionise", "seamless"), no
em-dashes, no promises of results.
